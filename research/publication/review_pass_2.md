# Second review pass (M4): arguing against the preferred reading

Date: 2026-11-25. Internal quality control by the AI assistant, not peer review. Object: `research/paper/manuscript.md` as first synthesised in M4, the three experiment reports and `research/publication/m4_interpretation.md`.

**Preferred reading under challenge.** "The frozen rejection of H0 is a robust difference in order between A1 and A0 (A1 at w_c 0.5 sustains much less alignment), not a difference in recovery; the design cannot measure A1's recovery." The pass also re-examines the original directional reading ("A1 recovers worse") that the preferred reading replaced.

Each item states the challenge, the evidence, whether it holds, and what changed.

## 1. Strongest alternative readings

**1.1 "A1 is the more resilient rule."** X1 in the 8 orderable cells is +0.012 [-0.002, 0.026], X4 shows A1 losing less order at the shock (-0.017 [-0.026, -0.008]), and the cell-mean A0 change score is negative in 6 of the 8 orderable cells (for example -0.030 at rho 0.5, eta 0.1, r 1; exploratory per-cell values). One could argue that A1 is perturbed less and so is more resilient.
*Assessment.* Does not hold as a resilience claim. A1's pre-shock polarization is only about 0.03 to 0.17 above the random level, so it has little order to lose; a smaller drop is a floor effect. The X1 interval contains zero and lies inside the SESOI band. *Change.* The manuscript (Section 5.4) states the floor effect explicitly; no resilience claim is made. Added to the claims register as a qualified, exploratory row (CL-008) so the reading is visibly rejected rather than silently absent.

**1.2 "A1 recovers worse" (the original direction of H1's negative branch).** The frozen result is negative and narrow, and a reader of the abstract alone could take it as evidence that separation and cohesion hurt recovery.
*Assessment.* Does not hold: phi_pre A1 - A0 (-0.312 [-0.319, -0.304]) equals Delta within the CIs, and the exploratory change score is 0.004 [-0.004, 0.012]. *Change.* None needed beyond what the first draft already said; the abstract's last sentences state that the design could not measure A1's recovery.

**1.3 "The order gap is stationary."** The first draft called the gap a difference in "stationary order" in the title, abstract, discussion and conclusion. Rule R-BI checked only A0's pre-shock alignment; nothing checked that A1 had reached a steady state by t_d = 3000. A1 might still be slowly coarsening into fewer, larger clusters, and its long-run order could differ.
*Evidence.* A1's phi_post is close to its phi_pre in every cell (EXP-002 per-cell table), which rules out fast drift over steps 2900 to 3499, but not slow drift. The pre-freeze diagnostic EXPL-006 measured A1 only at T = 500. *Assessment.* Holds. "Stationary" is unverified. *Change.* The manuscript title, abstract, discussion and conclusion now say "sustained" or "pre-shock" order, and Section 7 adds a limitation that A1's stationarity was not checked (limitation 18).

## 2. Overgeneralisation

**2.1 "Separation and cohesion lower order."** The first draft's title attributed the effect to separation and cohesion in general. Only one rule family was tested: zonal priority of repulsion, r_s = 0.25 r, w_a 1, w_c in {0.25, 0.5, 1.0}, no turning-rate limit. At smaller w_c, or with weighted rather than prioritised repulsion, A1 may order.
*Assessment.* Holds. *Change.* Title changed to name "a zonal separation-cohesion rule"; the conclusion names the parameterisation; limitation 6 is cross-referenced in Section 7.

**2.2 Narrow CIs read as generality.** The pooled CI [-0.313, -0.302] reflects seed variation within 18 fixed cells under equal weights, not uncertainty over the space of densities, noises or radii. The pooled value also mixes two regimes (X2: -0.618 in orderable cells, -0.059 in flagged cells).
*Assessment.* Holds as a caution. *Change.* Section 4.3 now states that the CI is conditional on the 18 cells and their equal weights; Section 5.2 reports the regime split (already present as exploratory X2).

**2.3 EXP-003 robustness read as robustness everywhere.** S1 to S3 cover 4 of 18 cells, all at r = 1, two of which are cells where A0 is disordered.
*Assessment.* Already stated in Section 7 (coverage). No change.

## 3. Stale figures and numbers

- **Generated figures.** All three figures are the PNGs generated by `experiments/analyse_confirmatory.py` at the M3 analysis; the analysis files were not regenerated after, and their SHA-256 values are recorded in the artifact manifest. No stale copy exists in `research/paper/figures/` (the manuscript links the generated files directly). No change.
- **Planning quantities.** The per-directory generated `report/report.md` files contain a paired-difference section computed by the M2 report generator, which is not the frozen analysis. The EXP-002 report already says so. No manuscript number comes from those files (number audit). No change.
- **Random level.** The EXP-001 summary describes the random level as about 1/sqrt(N) = 0.071, while the exploratory X4 uses the expected value sqrt(pi) / (2 sqrt(N)) = 0.063. Both are standard approximations (root-mean-square and mean of the polarization of independent headings). *Change.* The manuscript states which one it uses and where.
- **Data manifest text.** `data/manifest.json` still says the simulator is "not yet implemented". Its SHA-256 is recorded in every confirmatory run's provenance, so editing it would break that link. *Change.* Recorded as a finding in the evidence audit; left unchanged for the owner to decide (it is a text note, not data).

## 4. Leakage and researcher degrees of freedom

- **Pre-freeze knowledge of the contrast.** Draft-design smoke values (A1 - A0 -0.480) were seen before the freeze, and the owner then chose w_c = 0.5 knowing that A1 did not order at w_c = 1. The choice was aimed at making A1 order, not at the contrast's sign, and it did not succeed. Disclosed (C4, DL-009). No change.
- **Burn-in rule.** R-BI used A0-only, shock-free runs on disjoint seeds, so it could not see the primary contrast. No change.
- **Post hoc analyses.** X1 to X5 were chosen after seeing the confirmatory results, specifically to probe the confound. They could have been chosen to produce a desired answer. Mitigation: all five are reported, including the ones that cut against the preferred reading (1.1 above), they use the frozen bootstrap, and none changes a verdict. The manuscript labels them exploratory everywhere they appear.
- **EXP-003 reference reading.** Fixed in writing before data (DL-012); both readings are reported and agree. No change.

## 5. Missing failed runs and selective reporting

- Every confirmatory directory has exactly one launch in its `run.log`, with "0 failed" and exit 0; the provenance tables have one row per planned run (1080, 160, 160, 160). There is no evidence of an aborted or repeated confirmatory attempt.
- All exploratory EXP-001 runs are listed in supplementary S1 with their counts; all had 0 failures.
- Re-hashing in M4: 1,584 files listed in the four `raw_manifest.json` files, 0 mismatched or missing.
- The flagged cells, the positive per-cell differences and the near-zero high-noise cells are reported in the manuscript, not only the pooled result.

## 6. Causal and mechanistic language

- The discussion proposes that repulsion with priority, inside tight clusters, keeps changing headings. This is not measured. *Change.* The sentence is labelled a hypothesis and Section 9 lists a measurement of it (fraction of agents in the repulsion zone per step) as future work.
- "A1 required more distance evaluations ... consistent with clustering": the cell-list count depends on local density, so this is an indirect indicator. Left as worded ("a sign of"), with no causal claim.
