## 5. Compute budget (planning estimate)

EXP-002: 540 runs of N = 200 agents for 1,000 steps; EXP-003: at most 320 runs. With a NumPy cell list, a step at N = 200 is expected to cost on the order of a millisecond, giving minutes per arm, well under the 4-hour and 2-hour caps. This is an assumption; EXP-001 measures it before the freeze. The demo runs a smaller swarm in a Web Worker.

## 8. Scope, exclusions and fallback

- In scope: 2-D simulation, three arms, one disturbance type in the confirmatory design, three registered experiments, static demo, reports and manuscript.
- Excluded: 3-D models, real robots, empirical animal data, learning-based controllers, communication delay models.
- Fallback if the budget is threatened: cut EXP-003 to S1 only, then reduce the EXP-002 grid to radius r = 1.0 (9 cells, 270 runs) before the freeze; cut demo polish before any evidence control.

## 9. Risks

1. Parameter cells where A0 is disordered before the shock (high noise, low density) make phi_post uninformative there; reported, not dropped.
2. Periodic-boundary band states may dominate some cells; S3 checks it.
3. The A1 parameterisation is one of many; results do not generalise to all zonal rules.
4. The pooled estimand averages over heterogeneous cells; sign changes across cells are possible and must be shown.
